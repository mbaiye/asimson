About Us

Quality, Safety & Purity for Your Water Needs

Asimson Life Products is a water solutions company based in Loburo, Ogun State, serving homes, businesses and investors across Nigeria. We deliver complete pure water factory setups — equipment, installation, training and NAFDAC registration support — alongside borehole drilling, water treatment and expert consultancy.

Whether you want to start a profitable sachet or bottled water business, or simply want clean, safe water at home, our team handles everything from the source to the tap.

  • Turnkey factory setup from scratch to production
  • Support on licensing & NAFDAC compliance
  • Professional borehole drilling & water treatment
  • On-site home water purification installation
